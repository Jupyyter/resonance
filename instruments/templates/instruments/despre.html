Our story

Resonance started in {{ companie.founded }} as a repair bench in a Bucharest flat, run by two musicians who were tired of new guitars arriving with high strings and sharp fret ends.

The repairs turned into a small shop, and the shop into this site. We still sell the way we started: every instrument goes through the workshop before it's packed.

The workshop

Guitars and basses get new strings, a neck adjustment, string height and intonation set, and their frets checked along the whole neck. Keyboards and electronic kits are switched on and tested on every key, pad and output.
