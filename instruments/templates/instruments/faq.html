Can I return an instrument?

Yes, within 14 days of delivery, in the condition and case it arrived in. If it arrived faulty, we pay for the return shipping.

What does "set up" mean?

Adjusting an instrument so it plays well: the curve of the neck, the height of the strings, the intonation, and the frets and electronics checked. Factory settings are a starting point, so we finish the job before it ships.

Can I try an instrument before buying it?

Yes, in the showroom during opening hours. Call ahead for a particular model and we'll have it tuned and waiting.

Do you ship outside Romania?

We ship across the European Union. The cost is shown before you pay.

Do you buy used instruments?

We take trade-ins after a look in the workshop. Bring the instrument in and we'll make an offer the same day.
