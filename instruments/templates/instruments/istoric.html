Shop history

From a repair bench to a shop with its own workshop, year by year.

  1. {{ companie.founded }}

    A repair bench in a Bucharest flat: setups, fret work and electronics for local players.

  2. 2020

    The first online sales, mostly second-hand guitars that had been through the bench.

  3. 2021

    A rented workshop in Sector 2, and the first new instruments from distributors.

  4. 2023

    Keyboards, synthesizers and a small drum room join the shop.

  5. 2025

    Rentals for gigs and recording sessions.

  6. 2026

    This website, with 3D models and recordings for every instrument.
