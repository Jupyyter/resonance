Look and listen before you buy

Each product page has a 3D model of the instrument. Turn it around, zoom in on the headstock or the controls, and check the finish from every side.

Next to it are recordings of that model, played clean and through an amplifier, so you can hear it before it arrives.

How an order works

  1. You order

    Pay online by card or on delivery. We confirm by email the same day.

  2. We set it up

    The instrument goes through the workshop: strings, neck, action, intonation and electronics.

  3. It ships in a case

    Packed in its case or a padded box, with a note listing what we adjusted.

  4. You have 14 days

    Play it at home. If it isn't right, send it back within 14 days of delivery.
